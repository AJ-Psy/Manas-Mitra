# Manas Mitra

### Exploring the Intersection of Psychology, Artificial Intelligence & Education

Welcome to Manas Mitra!

Manas Mitra is an initiative exploring how Psychology, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Education can come together to create meaningful learning experiences and practical digital tools.

## Our Focus Areas

- **Psychology:** Understanding human behaviour, cognition, emotions, and learning.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Exploring AI tools, intelligent applications, and agentic workflows.
- **Education:** Developing interactive learning resources and technology-supported educational experiences.
- **Research:** Exploring research tools, data analysis, and evidence-based approaches.

## Projects & Experiments

This repository serves as a space for developing and sharing:

- Interactive Psychology learning resources.
- AI-powered educational tools.
- Psychology research utilities.
- Digital learning experiments.
- Open-source educational projects.

## Vision

To explore innovative ways of connecting human understanding with emerging technologies and making knowledge more accessible.
